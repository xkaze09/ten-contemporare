Jonet Carpio

Oil on Canvas — Figurative / Historical

Jonet Carpio's paintings are saturated with reference — literary, historical, mythological — but they do not announce this. The surface appears approachable until you look longer, and then the density of what is embedded in a single canvas becomes apparent.

He works in oil, slowly. His figurative practice places the human body in situations that carry the weight of narrative without illustrating it. You understand that something has happened, or is about to happen, without being told what it is.

His references include Baroque Spanish painting, Philippine revolutionary history, and the literature of José Rizal. None of this is cited explicitly. It is metabolised into image.

"The painting should contain more than the viewer first sees. If you get everything in thirty seconds, I have failed."
