Profile

Summer Pasana

Mixed Media — Spatial / Textural

Summer Pasana came to visual art from interior design, and the spatial intelligence that training gave her is entirely visible in her work. She approaches the flat surface the way an architect approaches a room — every plane is a decision about where the eye should rest, and where it should be refused.

Her mixed media practice draws on materials that carry their own history: raw linen, wax, tissue paper, sand collected from specific beaches. The surface of a Pasana work is a record of process, but not an expressive one. It is considered. She spends months on a single piece, building up and removing until the work contains exactly what it should.

Her work resists quick reading. It offers more to the viewer who is willing to stand still in front of it.

"I want the surface to do what a good room does — make you aware of the quality of your own attention."
