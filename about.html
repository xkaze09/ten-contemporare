About the Gallery

Filipino art, presented
without apology.

Ten Contemporare was established to give Filipino contemporary art the kind of presentation it receives in international gallery contexts — deliberate, well-lit, and without apology.

We have two physical spaces in Metro Manila. We hold four to six exhibitions per year. We do not rush the work, and we do not rush the collector.

"The artists we represent are making work that will matter in twenty years. That is the only criterion."

Our locations in Pasig (Kapitolyo) and Quezon City (Katipunan) are open Tuesday through Sunday. Visits by appointment are welcome for collectors who prefer a private showing.

What We Stand For

Our exhibitions are small. Our roster is selective. Every artist we represent is chosen because their work says something that cannot be said otherwise. We do not add artists to the roster because of market momentum or institutional endorsement. We add them because the work demands it.

The gallery was founded on a single conviction: that Filipino contemporary art deserves the same conditions of serious presentation that work from New York, London, or Berlin receives. Not as a performance of internationalism, but as a basic act of respect toward the work itself.

We believe collectors should take time. We believe artists should be given space to develop a body of work before the market is asked to evaluate it. We believe the conversation between a collector and a work of art is private, and our role is to create the conditions for that conversation to happen without interruption.

This is not a store. It is a gallery. The distinction matters to us.

Our Approach

Slow looking.
Considered collecting.

We believe that the best collecting decisions are made slowly. Not because we want to create artificial scarcity, but because a work of art that has been looked at seriously over time has a different relationship to the collector than one acquired impulsively.

Our acquisition process is built around conversation. We want to understand who you are as a collector, what you are trying to build, and what the work you are considering means to you. This is not a qualification process — it is an attempt to make sure that the relationship between you and the work has a solid foundation.

If you are acquiring for the first time, we will walk you through the process as carefully as we would with a serious long-term collector. There is no hierarchy of seriousness here. There is only the work.
