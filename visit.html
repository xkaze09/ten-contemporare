Private Viewings

The gallery is also available by appointment.

Collectors who prefer to view works privately — outside of public gallery hours, with focused time in front of a specific piece — are welcome to request a private viewing. We accommodate this regularly.

Private viewings can be arranged at either space. Simply send us a message with your preferred dates and the works or artists you would like to spend time with.

Getting Here

Pasig — Kapitolyo

Nearest MRT: Shaw Boulevard Station (Blue Line)
From Shaw: 10–15 min by rideshare or tricycle

Parking available at Pioneer Center.
Rideshare drop-off: Pioneer Street entrance.

Quezon City — Katipunan

Nearest LRT: Katipunan Station (Line 2)
From station: 5 min walk to St. Ignatius

Street parking along Katipunan Avenue.
Rideshare drop-off: St. Ignatius corner.
